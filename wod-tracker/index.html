Log the lift. Run the clock. Keep the record.

WOD Tracker is a training log for CrossFit, weightlifting and hybrid athletes. 81 benchmark WODs, timers for EMOM, AMRAP, Tabata and For Time, and a personal record for every movement you train. It works offline, and your data stays on your phone unless you back it up.

Inside the app

Five screens, one job each

Nothing here is a mockup. These are the app's own screens, the ones you land on between sets.

Log

Lifts

Every set, with weight, reps and a note. The last time you trained a movement is the first thing you see.

Clock

Timer

EMOM, AMRAP, For Time and Tabata, with audio cues and a time cap. Quick rest timers for the gaps between sets.

Library

Benchmarks

81 benchmark WODs come with the app. Pick one and start its timer from the same card.

Record

Lift stats

Personal record, total volume, average weight and set count for any movement, with the whole history as a chart.

Read

Analytics

Training load, consistency and the lifts that have stopped moving. It says when to deload instead of leaving you to guess.

The library

81 workouts you already know by name

The whole benchmark library ships inside the app. No download, no subscription, no signal needed at the box.

On the wrist, and off the grid

It follows you to the rig

Start a workout, log sets and run a rest timer from your watch. Sets you log while the phone is out of range are queued and sent across when it comes back.

  • Offline Everything works with no signal. Basements and garage gyms included.
  • Private Training data lives on your device. Nothing is uploaded unless you turn on backup yourself.
  • Health Workouts sync to Apple Health, so the rest of your health picture stays complete.
  • 18 languages The app is translated into 18 languages, with store listings in 22.

Plans

Logging is free, and stays free

Two paid tiers add the things that need a server or a subscription behind them. Prices are shown in the store for your country.

Free

Everything you need to keep a training log.

  • Unlimited lifts, sets and history
  • All 81 benchmark WODs
  • EMOM, AMRAP, Tabata and For Time timers
  • Automatic personal records and 1RM
  • Three workout templates
  • Apple Watch and Wear OS

Premium

For athletes who plan blocks rather than sessions.

  • Unlimited templates
  • Multi-week programs
  • Advanced analytics and plateau detection
  • Cloud backup and restore
  • Data export
  • Apple Health sync and body composition
  • Warm-up generator and recovery dashboard

PRO

Everything in Premium, plus coaching between sessions.

  • AI coach for suggestions and post-workout insight
  • Video demonstrations for 100+ movements
  • Live Activity on the lock screen
  • Form check

Subscriptions are handled by the App Store and Google Play, and can be cancelled there at any time.

Engineering

How it is built

One Kotlin Multiplatform codebase holds the data and domain layers. Each platform keeps its own native UI, so nothing feels ported.

  • Shared Kotlin Multiplatform·Room·Koin·Coroutines and Flow·Clean Architecture with use cases
  • Android Jetpack Compose·Material 3·WorkManager·Wear OS companion
  • iOS SwiftUI·SKIE for Kotlin interop·WidgetKit and ActivityKit·watchOS companion with HealthKit
  • Services Firebase Auth and Firestore·RevenueCat·Gemini 2.5 Flash for the coach
  • Delivery Fastlane·GitHub Actions·Detekt·Maestro flows·unit tests on both platforms
