Apple Watch and Wear OS

It follows you
to the rig.

Start a workout, log sets and run a rest timer from your wrist, with a buzz for every interval. Sets you log while the phone is out of range are queued and sent across when it comes back.

  • Timers on the wrist.

    EMOM, AMRAP, For Time and Tabata, plus a rest timer for the gaps between sets.

  • Heart rate, never stored.

    Shown while you train. The app doesn't keep it or upload it. On Apple Watch, Apple Health keeps it as it does for any workout.

  • Start from the phone.

    Begin a benchmark on the watch from your phone, and the result lands in your log.

Around the workout

Private by default.
Smart when you ask.

Works with no signal. Your data stays on your phone.

Basements and garage gyms included. Nothing is uploaded unless you turn on backup or ask the AI coach, and you can export your whole training history as CSV or JSON whenever you want, on any plan.

PRO

An AI coach that has read your log.

Workout suggestions from your logged history, post-workout insight and recovery recommendations. It asks before it sends anything, and it's general guidance, not medical advice.

Apple Health, kept complete.

Workouts sync to Apple Health, so the rest of your health picture stays in one place.

Premium

Back up, restore, change phones.

Cloud backup and auto-sync under your own Apple or Google sign-in are Premium. Restoring a backup and exporting your history are free on every plan, so a new phone is one restore away.

Plans

Logging is free,
and stays free.

Two paid tiers add the things that need a server or a subscription behind them. Prices are shown in the store for your country.

Free

Everything you need to keep a training log.

  • Unlimited lifts, sets and history
  • All 81 benchmark WODs
  • EMOM, AMRAP, Tabata and For Time timers
  • Automatic personal records and 1RM
  • Three workout templates
  • Apple Watch and Wear OS
  • Data export and restore
  • Live Activity on the lock screen (iPhone)

Premium

For athletes who plan blocks rather than sessions.

  • Everything in Free
  • Unlimited templates
  • Multi-week programs
  • Advanced analytics and plateau detection
  • Cloud backup and auto-sync
  • Apple Health sync and body composition
  • Warm-up generator and recovery dashboard
  • Timer Studio for the full-screen clock
  • Voice logging, supersets and circuits
  • Alternate app icons

PRO

Everything in Premium, plus coaching between sessions.

  • Everything in Premium
  • AI coach for suggestions and post-workout insight
  • Animated exercise demos
  • Form check for your lifts

A free trial applies when the store offers one for your account. Subscriptions are handled by the App Store and Google Play, and can be cancelled there at any time.
