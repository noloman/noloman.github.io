2. What Kuokka is

Kuokka is an independent client for Hacker News. It reads the public Hacker News API and adds reading tools on top: Radar watchlists, on-device summaries, reader mode, offline saving, comment navigation, widgets, bookmarks and search.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Y Combinator. "Hacker News" and related marks belong to their owner and are used here only to describe what the app reads.

3. No account

Kuokka has no sign-up and no login. Nothing you do in the app is tied to an identity we hold, and there are no credentials for you to keep safe with us.

4. Premium

Premium is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It does not renew and it will not charge you again. It removes ads, lifts the cap on summaries, and unlocks full Radar, For You recommendations, offline reading, comment digests and advanced search.

  • The price is shown in the app before you buy and varies by region and currency.
  • Billing runs through the App Store or Google Play. Neither we nor the app sees your payment details.
  • Premium is tied to the store account that bought it. Use "Restore purchases" in the app after reinstalling or moving to a new device.

5. Refunds

Refunds are handled by whichever store processed the purchase, under that store's policy and the consumer rights of your region — Apple through reportaproblem.apple.com, Google through the Play Store's order history. We cannot issue a refund directly, but if something is broken, write to us and we will help.

6. Content from other people

Stories, links and comments in Kuokka are written and submitted by Hacker News users. We do not select, edit, endorse or verify any of it, and we cannot vouch for its accuracy or availability.

Summaries and comment digests are produced automatically. They condense; they can be wrong, and they can miss the point of a piece. Treat them as a way into an article, not a replacement for reading it.
