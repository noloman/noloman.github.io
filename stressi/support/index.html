Support

Last updated: April 15, 2026

In short: Stressi works best when your Apple Health or Health Connect data is fresh and complete. If something looks wrong, start with a refresh and a permission check.

01Common fixes

  • Open Stressi and pull to refresh, or use the manual refresh action.
  • Check that Apple Health or Health Connect permissions are still granted.
  • Make sure your wearable companion app is writing heart rate, HRV, sleep, and workouts.
  • Wait until enough recent data exists for Stressi to establish a baseline.

02If your Body State looks incomplete

Stressi depends on the data available on your device. If HRV, sleep, or workout records are missing, the app shows lower-confidence guidance or missing-signal indicators instead of guessing.

03Subscription help

Stressi Pro purchases are managed by the App Store on iPhone or Google Play on Android. You can restore purchases from the paywall, or manage your subscription from your device's subscription settings.

04Contact

For product support, bug reports, or billing questions, contact manulorenzop+stressi@gmail.com.

Please include your device model, platform, app version, and a short description of the issue.

Bug reports can also be filed publicly at github.com/noloman/stressi/issues.
