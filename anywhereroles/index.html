How it works

Four steps, one app

Most job apps stop at search. AnywhereRoles covers the whole loop — from the first listing you open to the follow-up you nearly forgot.

01

Discover

Browse remote roles pulled from multiple sources, with source badges and a direct link to the original posting when you're ready to apply.

02

Shortlist

Cut the list down by salary, category, company, tags, region, date and source — or describe what you want in plain language and let the app build the filters.

03

Track

Save roles with notes, tags and folders, then move them through applied, interviewing, offer or rejected with reminders for the next step.

04

Learn

Salary insights, market statistics and company pages with hiring velocity and remote-policy signals help you compare offers before you commit.

Features

More than a list of jobs

The parts of a job search that usually end up in a spreadsheet are built into the app.

Search & filters

  • Title, company, skill or keyword search
  • Salary, category, source, tag, company, region and date filters
  • Reusable filter presets and saved searches
  • Plain-language filters turned into editable criteria

Fit & trust signals

  • Remote-fit scoring from your saved preferences
  • Timezone and region compatibility for "worldwide" claims
  • Flags for stale posts, likely duplicates and apply-domain mismatches
  • Quality thresholds to hide low-signal listings

Saved jobs

  • Notes, tags and folders on every saved role
  • Offline access to anything you've saved
  • CSV and JSON export
  • Optional cloud sync when you sign in

Alerts

  • Alerts for the categories and searches you care about
  • "Why this matched" explanations on every hit
  • Natural-language alert builder
  • Push notifications and background alert checks on Android

Application pipeline

  • Statuses from saved through to offer or rejection
  • Local follow-up reminders
  • Applied dates and application URLs kept with the role
  • Weekly briefing on pipeline health and next actions

Market insight

  • Salary insights and job market statistics
  • Company pages with recent hiring velocity
  • Remote-policy classification and salary transparency
  • Popular technologies and source trends

Platforms

One codebase, two native apps

Domain logic, networking, persistence and presentation live in a shared Kotlin Multiplatform module. Each platform keeps its own native UI and its own platform conventions.

Android

  • Jetpack Compose and Material 3
  • Dynamic colour and theme settings
  • Multiple list density modes
  • Push notifications and a home screen widget
  • Optional Google sign-in

iOS

  • Native SwiftUI throughout
  • Saved jobs, filters, onboarding and preferences
  • Application-status surfaces
  • Share extension and widgets
  • Sign in with Google or Apple

Shared

  • Kotlin Multiplatform data, domain and presentation layers
  • Ktor networking with Kotlinx Serialization
  • Room persistence and local-first saved jobs
  • Koin dependency injection
  • Firebase App Check on every API request

Clean architecture across data, domain and presentation, with Kotlin Flow driving state on both platforms — so a feature shipped once behaves the same in Compose and in SwiftUI.
